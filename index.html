Resume Writing Tips

Expert advice to create a winning resume

Keep It Concise

Limit your resume to 1-2 pages. Recruiters spend only 6-7 seconds on initial screening. Make every word count.

Use Action Verbs

Start bullet points with strong action verbs: Achieved, Developed, Managed, Created, Improved, Led, etc.

Quantify Achievements

Include numbers and metrics: "Increased sales by 35%" is better than "Improved sales performance".

Tailor for Each Job

Customize your resume for each application. Match keywords from the job description to pass ATS systems.

Proofread Carefully

Spelling and grammar errors are deal-breakers. Use spell-check and have someone review your resume.

Highlight Skills

Include both hard skills (technical) and soft skills (communication, leadership) relevant to the position.
