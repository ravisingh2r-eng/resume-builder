Frequently Asked Questions

Is Resume Builder really free?

Yes! Resume Builder is 100% free to use. You can create, customize, and download unlimited resumes without any charges or hidden fees.

Do I need to create an account?

No account required! You can start creating your resume immediately. Your data is saved locally in your browser.

How do I save my resume?

Your resume is automatically saved in your browser's local storage. You can also download it as a PDF for safekeeping.

Are the resumes ATS-friendly?

Yes! All our templates are designed to be compatible with Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) used by employers.

Can I use Resume Builder on mobile?

Absolutely! Resume Builder works perfectly on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Use the contact form above and select "Report a Bug" or "Feature Request" as the subject. We appreciate your feedback!
