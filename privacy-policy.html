Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 23, 2025

Quick Summary: We respect your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use Resume Builder. We use cookies and third-party services (Google AdSense, Google Analytics) to improve your experience and show relevant ads.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Resume Builder ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website https://yoursite.com and use our resume building services.

By using Resume Builder, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

When you use Resume Builder, we may collect the following information that you voluntarily provide:

  • Resume Content: Name, contact information, work experience, education, skills, and other information you include in your resume
  • Account Information: Email address (if you create an account)
  • Contact Form Data: Name, email, and message when you contact us
  • Feedback: Any feedback or suggestions you provide

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information about your device and browsing behavior:

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device type
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, click patterns, features used
  • Location Data: Approximate geographic location based on IP address
  • Cookies and Tracking: We use cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 5)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide Services: Generate, edit, and download your resume
  • Improve User Experience: Analyze usage patterns to enhance our features
  • Personalization: Remember your preferences and customizations
  • Communication: Respond to your inquiries and provide support
  • Analytics: Understand how users interact with our website
  • Advertising: Display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with legal obligations and prevent misuse

4. How We Store Your Information

4.1 Local Storage

Your resume data is stored locally in your browser using browser localStorage. This means:

  • Your resume data stays on your device and is not uploaded to our servers
  • We do not have access to your resume content
  • Your data is private and secure
  • Clearing your browser data will delete your saved resumes

4.2 Data Retention

Since your resume data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. We do not retain your resume data on our servers. Analytics and usage data collected through Google Analytics is retained according to Google's data retention policies (typically 14-26 months).

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

5.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. We use cookies to enhance your experience and collect analytics data.

5.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality
    • Saving your resume data locally
    • Remembering your preferences
  • Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics): Help us understand how visitors use our website
    • Page views and user behavior
    • Traffic sources
    • Popular features
  • Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense): Used to display personalized advertisements
    • Interest-based advertising
    • Frequency capping
    • Ad performance measurement

5.3 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Block All Cookies: Most browsers allow you to refuse all cookies
  • Delete Cookies: You can delete existing cookies from your browser
  • Third-Party Cookies: You can specifically block third-party cookies

Note: Blocking cookies may affect website functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

6.1 Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other websites.

Important: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Opt-Out: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Google AdSense Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6.2 Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to analyze website traffic and user behavior. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous information such as:

  • Number of visitors
  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on site
  • Geographic location (country/city)
  • Device and browser information

IP Anonymization: We have enabled IP anonymization in Google Analytics, which anonymizes your IP address.

Google Analytics Opt-Out: You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6.3 Font Awesome & Google Fonts

We use Font Awesome and Google Fonts to display icons and fonts. These services may collect usage data.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Google AdSense, Google Analytics (as described above)
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly agree to share information

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

  • HTTPS Encryption: Secure data transmission (when enabled)
  • Local Storage: Resume data stays on your device
  • No Server Storage: We don't store your resume content on our servers
  • Regular Updates: We keep our security practices up to date

Note: No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Privacy Rights

9.1 GDPR Rights (European Users)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Restriction: Limit how we use your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw previously given consent

9.2 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at: privacy@yoursite.com

10. Children's Privacy

Resume Builder is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us to have it removed.

11. International Data Transfers

Our website may be accessed from countries around the world. If you access our service from outside India, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in India or other countries where our service providers operate.

12. Do Not Track Signals

Our website does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals from browsers. However, you can control cookies and tracking through your browser settings and the opt-out links provided in this policy.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Posting a notice on our homepage
  • Sending an email notification (if you have an account)

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@yoursite.com
  • Support: support@yoursite.com
  • Website: https://yoursite.com
  • Terms: Terms of Service

15. Your Consent

By using Resume Builder, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of our services.
