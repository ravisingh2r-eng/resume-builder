Expert Tips for Your Fashion Designer Resume

Highlight Relevant Skills

Focus on Fashion Design, Sketching, Pattern Making and other industry-specific skills. Use these keywords to pass ATS screening.

Quantify Your Achievements

Use numbers, percentages, and metrics. For example: "Increased efficiency by 30%" or "Managed team of 10 people".

Keep It Concise

1-2 pages maximum. Focus on your most recent and relevant experience. Recruiters spend only 6 seconds on initial scan.

Make It ATS-Friendly

Use standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman), avoid images in main content, and use clear section headings.
