General Questions

Is Resume Builder really free?

Yes, Resume Builder is 100% free to use. You can:

  • Create unlimited resumes
  • Access all 560+ templates
  • Customize every section
  • Download as PDF
  • Use all features without restrictions

No credit card required, no hidden fees, no subscriptions.

Do I need to create an account?

No account required! You can start creating your resume immediately without signing up. Your resume data is saved locally in your browser for privacy and convenience.

How many templates are available?

We offer 560+ professional resume templates across 56+ job categories:

  • 10 unique designs per category
  • Pre-filled with industry-specific examples
  • ATS-friendly formatting
  • Modern and classic styles

You can create a professional resume in just 2 minutes! Our templates come with pre-filled examples, so you only need to:

Features & Functionality

Are the resumes ATS-friendly?

Yes! All our templates are designed to be compatible with Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). They feature:

  • Standard fonts (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman)
  • Clean, simple formatting
  • Proper section headings
  • No images or graphics in main content
  • Text-based design (not image-based)
Can I customize the templates?

Yes! All templates are fully customizable. You can edit:

  • Personal details (name, contact, summary)
  • Work experience (company, role, responsibilities)
  • Education (degree, university, year)
  • Skills (add/remove skills)
  • Additional sections (certifications, projects, languages)

Changes appear in real-time preview.

Can I download my resume as PDF?

Yes! You can download your completed resume as a high-quality PDF file with one click. The PDF format:

  • Looks professional on any device
  • Maintains formatting
  • Can be easily emailed to employers
  • Prints perfectly
Can I use Resume Builder on mobile?

Absolutely! Resume Builder works perfectly on:

  • Smartphones (iOS, Android)
  • Tablets (iPad, Android tablets)
  • Desktop computers
  • Laptops

The interface is fully responsive and optimized for all screen sizes.

Technical Questions

How do I save my resume?

Your resume is automatically saved in your browser's local storage as you work. For permanent backup:

  1. Download your resume as PDF
  2. Save the PDF to your device
  3. Consider backing up to cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox)

Note: Clearing browser data will delete saved resumes, so always download a PDF copy.

What browsers are supported?

Resume Builder works on all modern browsers:

  • Google Chrome (recommended)
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Opera

We recommend using the latest browser version for best performance.

Why isn't my resume saving?

If your resume isn't saving, try these solutions:

  • Check if cookies/local storage is enabled in your browser
  • Make sure you're not in incognito/private mode
  • Clear browser cache and try again
  • Try a different browser
  • Download as PDF to save manually

If the issue persists, contact us.

Can I print my resume?

Yes! You can print your resume in two ways:

  1. Download as PDF first (recommended) - Download the PDF and print from your PDF viewer for best results
  2. Print directly from browser - Use browser's print function (Ctrl+P)

We recommend printing the downloaded PDF for consistent formatting.

Account & Data Privacy

Is my data secure?

Yes, your data is completely secure. Here's why:

  • Your resume is stored locally on your device
  • We never access, store, or share your personal information
  • No data is sent to our servers
  • You have full control over your data

Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I delete my data?

Yes! Since your data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it anytime by:

  • Clearing your browser's local storage
  • Clearing browser cache and cookies
  • Using browser's "Clear browsing data" feature

Your data will be permanently deleted from your device.

Will you sell my information?

No, never! We:

  • Don't store your resume data on our servers
  • Don't sell any personal information
  • Don't share your data with third parties
  • Only collect anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics)

Your privacy is our top priority.

Can I access my resume from different devices?

Since your resume is stored locally in your browser:

  • Same device, same browser: Yes, your resume will be available
  • Different device or browser: No, you'll need to create it again or upload your PDF

Solution: Download your resume as PDF and keep it in cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox) to access from any device.
