Our Story

Resume Builder was born out of a simple observation: creating a professional resume shouldn't be difficult or expensive. We noticed that many job seekers struggle with:

  • Finding the right template for their industry
  • Knowing what to include in each section
  • Formatting their resume properly for ATS systems
  • Paying high prices for resume services

We decided to solve these problems by creating a completely free platform that combines beautiful templates with practical guidance. Our team spent months researching ATS requirements, interviewing hiring managers, and analyzing successful resumes to create templates that actually work.

Today, Resume Builder serves thousands of job seekers across India and beyond, helping them land their dream jobs.
